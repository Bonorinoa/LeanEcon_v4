## AI reviewer (DECISION_LOG 31; docs/gate3/08-reviewer-policy.md)

- `review` / `gap-ack` / `axiom-approve` accept human **or AI**.
- Identity always required (`--reviewer` / `LEANECON_REVIEWER_ID`).
- `--reviewer-kind human|ai|auto` (default auto). Auto-infers AI for
  `^(ai|hermes|hermes-agent|leanecon-ai)([:_-].+)?$` (case-insensitive)
  via `leanecon.reviewer_policy`.
- Explicit kind overrides inference; records store `reviewer_kind`.
- `none_noted` still requires `--acknowledge-none-noted` for **both** kinds.
- CTO remains accountable; AI is authorized agent. Interpret/formalize
  models remain drafting aids and never self-certify meaning.
